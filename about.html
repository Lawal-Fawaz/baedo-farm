OUR FARM

The company

Growing from a shared respect for the land.

BAEDO INTEGRATED FARM is an agricultural business focused on large-scale oil palm cultivation and production.

Our work is centered on the plantation: caring for the crop, supporting the harvest, and building an integrated farm operation. We believe agriculture should create value while keeping the land and the people connected to it in view.

This page introduces the principles that guide our work and the direction we hope to grow in.

Our commitment

Big-picture ambition.
Ground-level care.

Large-scale palm oil agriculture calls for a long view and a strong connection to day-to-day farm work. BAEDO INTEGRATED FARM is committed to developing its plantation and production operation with care, consistency, and respect for the people and land involved.

We are focused on building a farm operation that can grow thoughtfully over time. Our commitment is an ongoing one, shaped by the work ahead and the choices we make along the way.
